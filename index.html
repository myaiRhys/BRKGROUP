What we do

Four services, done properly.

A smaller client might only ever need the first. A larger one might only need the second. Both are equally the business. Take what you need.

  1. websites

    Websites & digital presence

    Affordable, professional sites that work on mobile and say the right thing. Google Business, social profiles and directory listings set up and pointing the right way. The natural place for a smaller business to start.

    Best entry point for smaller companies

  2. software

    Custom software & app builds

    Internal tools, booking, tracking and job-management systems, client-facing apps — built around how your operation actually runs, not forced into off-the-shelf software. Where most of the larger-company work lives.

    Built for larger or more complex operations

  3. growth

    Business development & growth consulting

    Positioning, target-market strategy, pricing and outreach systems. The thinking behind the build — figuring out who you're actually for and how the work should come in, before a line of anything gets made.

    Strategy, not just delivery

  4. retainer

    Ongoing retainer & pipeline management

    For clients who want it managed after launch: leads and pipeline kept moving, iterative development, and a steady hand on the systems so they keep earning their keep instead of going stale.

    For clients who want it run, not handed over

About

A studio that builds things.

BRK Group is a software and digital consultancy in the plain sense: we help companies get their online presence and their internal systems in order — and keep them working. From an affordable first website to custom software built for the way you actually operate.
